Introducing Tokenized Sapphires: A New Era for Real-World Assets

#digitalassets #realworldassets #RWA #tokenization #tokenizeddiamonds
April 7, 2025 - 3 min read

At Tiamonds, we’re on a mission to make rare, real-world assets more accessible, transparent, and secure through blockchain and today, we’re incredibly proud to announce our latest milestone: the launch of tokenized sapphires, in exclusive partnership with Berr & Partners.

This isn’t just another product drop, it’s a bold step forward in bridging the world of fine gemstones with the future of digital ownership.

Rare Sapphires, Now on the Blockchain

We’ve worked closely with Elke Berr and her expert team at Berr & Partners, a globally respected firm in the gemstone industry, to source natural, unheated sapphires of the highest quality—directly from the famous mines of Sri Lanka.

In partnership with Berr & Partners, we’re offering those rare gems representing just 2% of the total supply, and now, you can own one as a tokenized real-world asset (RWA).

Once selected, each gemstone goes through a thorough certification process to ensure traceability, authenticity, and unmatched quality. From there, we tokenize them—transforming each sapphire into an asset-backed digital token that you can own, trade, or redeem at any time.“We have conducted an exhaustive sourcing process to handpick only the finest sapphires for this project, with our expertise and dedication, we provide collectors and investors with exclusive access to rare and exceptional gemstones, backed by full transparency.” says Elke Berr, Founder of Berr & Partners.

Your Sapphire, Your Token, Your Choice

Each tokenized sapphire is stored securely in 3rd party Swiss Vaults, insured by Lloyd’s of London, and fully redeemable whenever you choose. We’ve designed this marketplace to give you:

  • True digital ownership
  • On-chain verification and security
  • 24/7 global liquidity
  • Access to some of the rarest gemstones in the world

By putting these sapphires on-chain, we’re giving you the chance to own a piece of natural beauty—backed by the power of blockchain and our core philosophy: Freedom of Ownership.

“By bringing exceptional sapphires to the blockchain, we are merging tradition with innovation,this new offering enables gem lovers to access rare assets with full transparency, security, and liquidity.” says Steven Gaertner, our Director here at Tiamonds.

Why This Launch Matters

This launch goes beyond beautiful stones. It marks another step in our journey to expand what’s possible with tokenized real-world assets (RWAs). After our successful diamond tokenization, gold tokenization, we knew sapphires were the next chapter—adding depth, diversity, and brilliance to our portfolio.

We believe everyone should have the opportunity to invest in assets that were once reserved for the elite. With tokenized sapphires, we’re opening that door and doing it with world-class partners like Berr & Partners, who are the experts in the sapphire industry.

With tokenized sapphires, we’re not just offering gemstones—we’re offering a new way to experience ownership. Whether you’re a collector, an investor, or simply someone who values rare beauty, this is your opportunity to be part of something extraordinary.
